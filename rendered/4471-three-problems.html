#4471 · three problems, not one

#4471 was merged as one issue: dates parse month first. That explains most of its reports. It does not explain all of them. Read one by one, the reports hold three problems with three different fixes.

Known is not the same as understood. One fix will clear one of these three.

The export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Each report is counted once, by its id.

1 · parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant in the directory writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. Reports arrive from the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form, whose hint says DD/MM/YYYY. This is the cause #4471 was merged for, and the queued fix is the right fix for it.

2 · hint: the booking-v3 form teaches people the wrong order

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Tenants whose people write the day first type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form then reads the date their way. The parser fix does not touch this. The fix is the hint, per tenant locale. All of these reports come through the form, from the three tenants that run booking-v3.

3 · one-day shift: a day off, not a month swap

A month swap changes the day and the month. These reports are one day off with the month intact: a Tuesday that shows as Monday, a client in Lisbon who got Tuesday while a client in Toronto got Monday, an invite that arrived before the booking was made, and a month where every booking is a day off. That points at time zones and daylight saving, not at date order. The desk’s table filed these under parser because of their tenant, and footnoted them. They need their own ticket, an engineer to reproduce them, and an owner. The cause is not confirmed.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. These reports share a handful of subjects such as “Invite came on the wrong day” and “Wrong day on the invite”, so the desk could not tell three problems apart without reading the bodies. Intake’s own tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser: it called many hint reports parser and many parser reports hint. Place a report by its tenant and its channel, as src/lib/kb/causes.ts does, and not by its tag.

What to do

  • File one ticket per problem. Keep #4471 for parser only.
  • Fix the booking-v3 hint for day-first tenants; it is a separate change from the parser.
  • Give the one-day shift to an engineer, with its report ids from the desk notes.
  • Replace decision 0001 with a record that says when a subject match is not enough. A decision is never edited once accepted.
